# Business Requirements Document (BRD)
# Project Name
GlobalTech AI ITSM – GenAI & Agentic AI

# 1. Business Overview
GlobalTech Solutions is an organization with approximately 10,000 employees. 
The organization wants to improve IT Service Management through intelligent automation and AI-assisted decision-making.

# 2. Business Problem
Traditional ITSM processes require manual effort for:

. Incident classification and prioritization.
. Knowledge article identification.
. Recurring incident investigation.
. Change risk assessment.
. Approval and governance management.
# 3. Project Objectives
. Automate AI-assisted incident analysis.
. Identify recurring incidents and recommend Problem investigation.
. Assess Change risks.
. Provide knowledge-based troubleshooting.
. Introduce approval controls for high-risk actions.
. Maintain complete AI execution audit logs.
# 4. Project Scope
# Included
. Incident Management
. Problem Management
. Change Management
. AI Request Management
. AI Agent execution
. AI Governance
. AI Approval workflow
. AI Audit Logging
# Excluded from Current Implementation
. Live external LLM integration.
. Production deployment.
. Autonomous execution of real high-risk ITSM actions.
# 5. User Roles
. Employee
. Service Desk Analyst
. Manager
. Administrator
# 6. Functional Requirements
1. Employees can submit AI Requests.
2. AI agents analyze requests based on request type.
3. AI recommendations are generated.
4. High-risk actions require approval.
5. Approved actions are simulated.
6. Failed executions can be retried.
7. All important activities are logged.
8. Role-based access controls protect AI Requests.
# 7. Non-Functional Requirements
. Secure access control.
. Execution traceability.
. Error handling.
. Maintainability.
. Reliable audit logging.
# 8. Expected Outcome
A ServiceNow-based AI-assisted ITSM solution demonstrating agent orchestration, governance, approval workflows, recommendations, and auditability.
